# Tex Dex SRM — Supplier Email Template

**Use after the 9/28 Growth@ announcement.** This is your follow-up, not a replacement for Growth@. Keep it short. Do not paste the data sheet, L90D math, or negotiation ladder into this email.

Send from your own inbox. Personalize the first sentence. If Growth@ has not gone out yet, wait until Monday 9/28 so this lands as confirmation, not a surprise.

---

**Subject:** Quick note on how discounts will work starting November 9

Hi [First Name],

I wanted to flag a change coming **Monday, November 9** so you hear it from me as well as from our Growth@ note.

**What is changing**
Today, only the deepest discount applies. That often wipes out Everyday B2B pricing during events. Starting November 9, your **Everyday B2B discount will apply on top of live B2C promotional costs** (major shopping holidays and short-term promos). Flash Deals and other item-level deals will also apply on top of the live B2C promo. Everyday B2B and Flash Deals still **do not stack with each other** on the same item.

**What is not changing**
You still set Everyday B2B in Pricing Home / Partner Home, and changes still take effect immediately. B2C rooms (events, weekend rooms, superrooms, extended discount rooms) still do **not** stack with each other.

**What this means for you**
You no longer need to rebuild the same B2B discount in every source room for every event. Review your current Everyday B2B input before November 9 so it reflects the investment you actually want going forward.

Wayfair is also hosting a supplier webinar on **Tuesday, October 13 at 11am ET** ([register here](https://wayfair.zoom.us/webinar/register/WN_FGV9Bf0hT3e-w2aLyxkdzQ#/registration)), and Partner Home will show net cost more clearly in early October.

Happy to chat through these changes in our next call.

Thanks,
[Your name]
[Your title], Wayfair
